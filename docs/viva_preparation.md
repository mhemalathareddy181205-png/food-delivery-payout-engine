## 1. Key Evaluation Questions (Mandatory Viva Topics)

### Q1: Explain how discount funding changes the commission base.
**Answer:**
1. **Commission Base Principle**: Platform commission is intended to represent a percentage of the actual net revenue generated by the restaurant's food sales.
2. **Restaurant-Funded Discounts**: When a restaurant offers a discount (e.g. 10% off funded by restaurant), the restaurant absorbs the discount, reducing its gross food realization. Therefore, the commission base is reduced:
   $$\text{Commission Base} = \text{Gross Item Value} - \text{Restaurant-Funded Discount}$$
   Charging commission on the undiscounted gross would penalize the restaurant for funding promotions out of its own margin.
3. **Platform Coupons**: When a promotional coupon is funded by the platform (e.g. `WELCOME50` platform voucher), the platform subsidizes the discount to drive user acquisition. The restaurant was promised full item price. Consequently:
   - Platform coupons do **not** reduce the commission base (commission is calculated on full net item value).
   - The platform reimburses the restaurant for the exact coupon value: $\text{coupon\_reimbursement} = \text{platform\_coupon}$.
4. **Co-Funded Discounts (50:50)**:
   - The 50% restaurant share is treated as a restaurant discount and reduces the commission base.
   - The 50% platform share is treated as a platform coupon and does not reduce the commission base, but is reimbursed to the restaurant.
   - Any odd paisa (e.g. Rs 0.01) is assigned to the platform share to ensure the restaurant's deduction does not round up arbitrarily.

### Q2: Why is GST under Section 9(5) a deduction rather than a charge?
**Answer:**
1. **Statutory Definition**: Under Section 9(5) of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act 2017, Electronic Commerce Operators (ECOs like Swiggy, Zomato) are legally notified as the deemed supplier liable to discharge 5% GST directly to the government on restaurant services supplied through their digital platform.
2. **Deduction vs. Charge**:
   - A **charge** (such as the 18% GST on platform commission) is a fee or value-added tax for services provided *by the platform to the restaurant*. The platform retains the commission as its service revenue and collects GST on its service.
   - A **deduction** under Section 9(5) is a statutory withholding for remittance to the government. The customer pays the total order value to the platform. The platform must remit 5% of the gross food value directly to the state/central tax authorities on behalf of the transaction.
   - Therefore, the platform withholds/deducts this 5% tax from the restaurant's gross proceeds so that the tax liability is satisfied without double taxation or leaving the tax unremitted. The platform does *not* keep this money as revenue; it is purely an outward tax remittance.

### Q3: How does Section 194-O TDS work, and what happens at the Rs 5 lakh boundary?
**Answer:**
1. **Section 194-O Mandate**: E-commerce operators must deduct 1% Income Tax TDS on the gross amount of sales facilitated through their digital marketplace for domestic participants.
2. **Threshold Exemption**: Individual and HUF sellers whose annual gross merchandise value (GMV) on the platform does not exceed Rs 5,00,000 in a financial year (April 1 to March 31) are exempt from this deduction.
3. **Crossing the Boundary (Planted Scenario EC-03)**:
   - Cumulative GMV starts with `opening_fy_gmv` (facilitated between April 1 and the start of the payout stream).
   - Only delivered orders contribute to GMV (cancelled orders are excluded).
   - In week 2, RST-05 (opening GMV Rs 495,750.00) delivers ORD-0071 (Rs 1,700) -> GMV Rs 497,450 (no TDS); ORD-0084 (Rs 1,700) -> GMV Rs 499,150 (no TDS).
   - On Wednesday 2026-10-14, ORD-0095 (Rs 1,700) pushes cumulative GMV to Rs 500,850. Because cumulative GMV now exceeds Rs 5 lakh, 1% TDS (Rs 17.00) applies from that crossing transaction onward.

### Q4: How are order cancellations handled for customer vs restaurant?
**Answer:**
1. **Customer Cancellation (BR-06 & EC-04)**:
   - **Free Window ($\le$ 60 seconds)**: If customer cancels within 60 seconds of placement, it is classified as `CANCELLED_BY_CUSTOMER_FREE`. The customer receives a 100% refund. The restaurant has not started food prep, so restaurant net is Rs 0.00 and no commission or penalty is charged.
   - **Charged Cancellation (> 60 seconds)**: If cancelled after 60 seconds, it is classified as `CANCELLED_BY_CUSTOMER_CHARGED`. The customer is charged 100% because food preparation has begun. The restaurant is paid in full (net item value + allowed packaging + coupon reimbursement) minus platform commission and statutory taxes.
2. **Restaurant Cancellation (BR-06 & Trap 3)**:
   - If the restaurant cancels after accepting the order, the restaurant is penalized with a flat Rs 100 deduction (`penalty = 100.00`), and the customer refund is borne 100% by the restaurant (`refund_restaurant_share = customer_refund`). Restaurant net is `-100.00`.
   - If the restaurant cancels before acceptance, no penalty applies.

### Q5: How does the weekly ad spend deduction and debit carry-forward work?
**Answer:**
1. **Ad Budget Cap**: Deductions are strictly capped by the restaurant's weekly ad budget (`weekly_ad_budget`). Any unspent budget expires at week's end and never carries over.
2. **Zero-Floor Protection (EC-06)**: Ad spend deductions cannot push the restaurant's net weekly payout below zero.
3. **Debit Carry-Forward**: If a restaurant's available order earnings (`gross_payable`) are insufficient to cover the authorized ad spend, the unpaid portion carries forward to the following week as an outstanding debit (`debit_carried_forward`).
4. **Recovery Priority**: In subsequent weeks, available earnings first pay down carried-forward debits before recovering new ad spend.

### Q6: How are disputes handled across weekly payout cycles?
**Answer:**
1. **Dispute Exclusion (BR-10 & EC-07)**:
   - Orders with an open dispute at the time of payout are excluded from that week's statement (`orders_excluded_dispute = 1`).
   - The order is held in suspense until the dispute closes.
   - Once resolved, the order is settled in the payout week containing the dispute's closing timestamp.
2. **Planted Scenario EC-07**:
   - ORD-0067 was delivered Sunday 2026-10-11 at 23:35.
   - A dispute opened Sunday 23:59:00 and closed Tuesday 2026-10-13 at 08:00:00 (one hour before week 1 payout ran at 09:00).
   - Because the dispute closed on Tuesday Oct 13, which falls in the Mon–Sun week starting 2026-10-12, ORD-0067 is excluded from week 1 (2026-10-05) and settled in week 2 (2026-10-12).

## 2. Planted Edge Cases (EC-01 to EC-08) Defense Summary

| Edge Case | Condition Tested | System Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| **EC-01** | Restaurant onboarded exactly 60 days before order | Days 0–60 inclusive qualify for 12% introductory commission (RST-03 day 59 & day 60 = 12%; day 61 = 18%). |
| **EC-02** | Co-funded discount with packaging charge above Rs 30 cap | Packaging charge (Rs 45) is capped at Rs 30 with Rs 15 disallowed. Co-funded discount (Rs 120) splits 50:50; restaurant's Rs 60 reduces commission base. |
| **EC-03** | Section 194-O GMV crosses Rs 5 lakh midweek | RST-05 opening GMV Rs 495,750; crossing occurs on Wednesday 2026-10-14 (ORD-0095); 1% TDS applies starting with the crossing transaction. |
| **EC-04** | Customer cancellation at exactly 60 seconds | Cancellation at 60s is free (`CANCELLED_BY_CUSTOMER_FREE`, net 0); cancellation at 59s is free; cancellation after 60s is paid in full minus commission. |
| **EC-05** | Weekly average rating exactly 3.50 | Penalty uplift (+2.0pp) applies strictly below 3.50. An average rating of exactly 3.50 (RST-10 week 1) incurs 0.0pp uplift. |
| **EC-06** | Ad spend larger than available weekly payout | Payout floors at Rs 0.00; unpaid ad spend carries forward as debit (RST-01 week 2 spend Rs 4800 against small earnings). |
| **EC-07** | Dispute opened Sunday 23:59 and closed Tuesday 08:00 | ORD-0067 is excluded from week 1 statement (`orders_excluded_dispute = 1`) and settled in week 2 statement. |
| **EC-08** | Net item value exactly Rs 100 | Net item value strictly below Rs 100 incurs flat Rs 10 fee with 0 commission (ORD-0117 net Rs 95); net item value of exactly Rs 100 uses percentage commission (ORD-0116 net Rs 100). |

## 3. Planted Traps Defense Summary

1. **RST-09 Week 1 Uplift**: Average rating is 3.40 (< 3.50) -> triggers +2.0 percentage points uplift in week 2 (Standard rate increases from 18% to 20%).
2. **RST-08 Week 2 Suspension**: Average rating is 2.60 (< 3.00) -> restaurant suspended for week 3 (`suspended_next_week = true`). In week 3, order ORD-0148 is immediately marked `REJECTED`.
3. **Restaurant Cancellation Penalty**: Accepted restaurant cancellations (ORD-0076, ORD-0120, ORD-0155, ORD-0182, ORD-0188, ORD-0200) incur Rs 100 penalty + refund, yielding net `-100.00`.
4. **Invalid Refund Reason Rejection**: Order ORD-0167 receives a refund request with reason `COLD_FOOD`. The engine rejects this request because only `MISSING_ITEM`, `SPILLED`, and `LATE_DELIVERY` are valid under platform policy.
5. **Packaging Exactly Rs 30**: RST-04 charges packaging Rs 30.00 -> allowed in full with 0 disallowed.
6. **Rider App Retry Idempotency**: Order ORD-0060 receives duplicate `DELIVERED` events. The engine processes it idempotently once without double-crediting.

## 4. Stretch Goals Implementation (Extra Credit)

### Stretch Goal 1: Multi-Restaurant Orders with Per-Restaurant Split
- **Function**: `multi_restaurant_split(item_lines, discounts_by_rest, packaging_by_rest)` in `src/calculations.py`.
- **Capability**: Allows a single customer order to contain food items sourced from multiple different partner restaurants.
- **Settlement Logic**: The engine partitions line items by `restaurant_id`, calculates each restaurant's individual gross item value, applies per-restaurant packaging and discounts, computes individual commission bases, and settles with each partner independently.

### Stretch Goal 2: Dynamic Commission by Cuisine Demand
- **Function**: `dynamic_commission_by_cuisine(cuisine, base_rate, demand_multiplier)` in `src/calculations.py`.
- **Capability**: Models algorithmic surge/demand elasticity pricing for commission rates.
- **Settlement Logic**: Adjusts base commission rates using cuisine category elasticities (e.g. Biryani peak demand multiplier 1.05x to 1.15x) during high-demand operational windows, boosting platform take-rates while remaining transparent to restaurant managers.
