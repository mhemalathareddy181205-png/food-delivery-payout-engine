# Decision Log (PS-039)

The decisions below record implementation details and conventions confirmed by the official PS-039 Sample Data Pack and evaluation requirements.

| Decision Area | Implemented Behavior | Alternative Considered | Confirmation Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Introductory Commission Boundary** | Days 0 through 60 inclusive after onboarding qualify for 12% rate (day 60 inclusive). | End introductory rate strictly before day 60 (day 59). | Confirmed by planted scenario EC-01: RST-03 orders on day 59 (12%), day 60 (12%), day 61 (18%). |
| **Co-funded Discount & Packaging Cap** | Co-funded 50:50 splits equally; odd paisa to platform. Packaging is capped at Rs 30 with excess disallowed; commission is never charged on packaging. | Deduct full discount from restaurant or charge commission on packaging. | Confirmed by planted scenario EC-02: ORD-0040 packaging Rs 45 capped to Rs 30, Rs 120 co-funded discount reduces base by Rs 60. |
| **Financial Year / Section 194-O TDS** | FY starts April 1. Total GMV includes `opening_fy_gmv` plus delivered order gross GMV. When an order pushes cumulative GMV past Rs 5 lakh, 1% TDS applies from that crossing transaction onward. | Withhold only on transactions after the crossing order, or calculate TDS on marginal excess only. | Confirmed by planted scenario EC-03: RST-05 crossing on Wednesday 2026-10-14 (ORD-0095) incurs 1% TDS (Rs 17.00) from that crossing. |
| **Customer Cancellation Timing** | Cancellation within 60 seconds is free (`CANCELLED_BY_CUSTOMER_FREE`). Cancellation after 60 seconds is charged in full (`CANCELLED_BY_CUSTOMER_CHARGED`). | Free window strictly less than 60s (59s) or prorated cancellation fees. | Confirmed by planted scenario EC-04: ORD-0049 at exactly 60s is free; ORD-0050 at 59s is free; ORD-0056 (>60s) is charged in full. |
| **Rating Penalty Boundary** | Weekly average rating strictly below 3.50 adds 2.0pp uplift. An average rating of exactly 3.50 incurs no penalty (`0.0`). | Penalize at <= 3.50. | Confirmed by planted scenario EC-05: RST-10 week 1 ratings average exactly 3.50 and receive 0.0pp penalty. |
| **Ad Spend Deduction & Debit Carry** | Deduct ad spend up to weekly budget and available positive payout; ad spend never pushes payout below zero; unpaid ad spend carries as debit; unspent budget expires. | Allow ads to make payout negative or expire unpaid debit. | Confirmed by planted scenario EC-06: RST-01 week 2 spend Rs 4800 floors payout at 0.00; remainder carries as debit. |
| **Dispute Timing & Weekly Selection** | Orders with an open dispute are excluded from the delivery week statement and settle in the week the dispute closes. | Settle in original delivery week or cancel disputed orders. | Confirmed by planted scenario EC-07: ORD-0067 opened Sun 23:59, closed Tue 08:00 (week 2) -> excluded from week 1, settled in week 2. |
| **Minimum Order Fee Boundary** | Net item value strictly below Rs 100 incurs flat Rs 10 platform fee, 0 commission, 0 commission GST. Net item value of exactly Rs 100 uses percentage commission. | Charge both platform fee and percentage commission. | Confirmed by planted scenario EC-08: ORD-0116 net Rs 100 uses percentage commission; ORD-0117 net Rs 95 uses flat Rs 10 fee. |
| **Suspension of Low-Rated Restaurants** | Weekly average rating strictly below 3.00 suspends new orders in the following week. Orders placed while suspended are rejected with status `REJECTED`. | Silently drop orders or allow pending acceptance. | Confirmed by Trap 2: RST-08 week 2 rating 2.60 suspends week 3; ORD-0148 is marked REJECTED. |
| **Restaurant Cancellation Penalty** | Restaurant cancellation after acceptance incurs flat Rs 100 penalty; customer refund is borne by restaurant. | Exclude customer refund or refund platform fees. | Confirmed by Trap 3: All 6 accepted cancellations incur Rs 100 penalty. |
| **Unsupported Refund Reasons** | Refund requests with invalid reasons (such as `COLD_FOOD`) are rejected and excluded from refund calculations. | Default unknown reasons to platform or restaurant fault. | Confirmed by Trap 4: ORD-0167 refund line with COLD_FOOD is rejected. |
| **Duplicate Delivery Idempotency** | Duplicate `DELIVERED` events (rider app retries) are processed idempotently without error or double-counting. | Fail on duplicate delivery or re-credit restaurant. | Confirmed by Trap 6: ORD-0060 duplicate delivery handled cleanly. |
